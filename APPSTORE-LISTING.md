## Description
```
A square. A tower. One rule: keep going up.

Hopscraper is a precision platformer with tight controls, no ads, and no waiting. Jump, double jump, wall jump and drop through platforms across 41 hand-built levels, then keep going with speed courses, an endless tower, a daily climb, and a brutal Hard Mode.

FEATURES

41 hand-built levels across five worlds, ending at THE TOP.

Three stars per level: finish it, find all the gems, and beat the target time.

Hard Mode - every level again with one air jump, no checkpoints, and a new twist each time.

Speed Courses - four non-stop courses with bronze, silver and gold medals.

Endless - a tower that never stops, with lava rising behind you.

Daily Climb - one tower a day, the same one for everybody.

Marathon - every level back to back on a single timer.

Power Cards - optional boosts for story levels. Use one and the level simply isn't timed for a record, so the leaderboards stay honest.

A shop full of skins, hats and trails, plus trophies to chase and hidden notes to find.

Three optional bonus worlds if you want more: the Low-G Zone, the Gilded Vale and Monster Woods. You never need them to finish the story.

MADE FOR PLAYING ANYWHERE

No ads. No energy timers. No internet needed. Every level is built by hand and verified beatable.

Made by GamerCrafter.
```

## Review notes (for Apple's reviewer)
```
Hopscraper is a single-player platformer. No account or login is required and no data is collected.

In-app purchases: gem packs, cosmetic packs, three optional bonus worlds and an optional monthly subscription ("Hopscraper Club"). Restore Purchases is in the Store tab.

The Premium Wheel is a random cosmetic reward bought with gems. Its odds are shown on the wheel face and in an "Odds" popup, duplicates convert to gems, and the top prize is guaranteed within 40 spins.

All game content is included in the app; nothing is downloaded at runtime.
```
