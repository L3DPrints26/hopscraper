Support

Hopscraper by GamerCrafter

Contact

Something not working? Email gamercrafter1510@gmail.com and say which device you play on.

A purchase didn't show up

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Packs and the Club come back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Purchases can take a few seconds to arrive.

Cancel the Hopscraper Club

In the Shop's Club tab tap Manage / Cancel Subscription, or go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. You stay a member until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. Read the privacy policy.
