Privacy Policy

Hopscraper by GamerCrafter · Last updated September 2026

We don't collect personal data

Hopscraper does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts to create and nothing you do in the game is sent to us.

Your progress stays on your device

Your levels, stars, items and settings are saved only on your own device: in your browser's local storage on the web, or inside the app on iPhone and iPad. Deleting the app or clearing your browser data removes it.

No analytics, ads or trackers

The game contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Purchases and Game Center

In-app purchases and Game Center leaderboards are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple ID. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Update checks on the web

The web version only contacts its own website, to check whether a newer version of the game is available. No information about you is sent.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact the developer through the Hopscraper listing on the App Store.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
